Help, anytime

Production security checklist

Before taking online orders: deploy behind HTTPS/TLS; use a payment provider’s hosted/tokenized checkout and never store full card details; protect merchant and admin access with MFA and least privilege; validate and rate-limit order APIs; add privacy disclosures, secure backups, and monitoring. These are deployment requirements, not controls enabled by this static demo.

A little help goes a long way.

Have a question about a piece or an order? Send us a note and we’ll get back to you personally.

Contact Aurora →
Where do you deliver?

We’re based in Kapolei, Hawaii and welcome inquiries from customers in the United States, Canada, and nearby destinations. Shipping options and costs are confirmed with you before an order is finalized.

How do I choose the right size?

Choose your usual size when adding an item to your bag. Because fit and available sizes may vary by piece, please email us with the item name and your measurements so we can confirm sizing before you order.

What is your return policy?

Return eligibility and timing are confirmed directly with you before purchase. Please contact us about the specific item before placing an order; we’ll share the applicable terms for your order.

How can I check on an order?

Order status is handled personally at this time. Email 75auroracauilan@gmail.com or call (808) 364-2251 with your name and order details for an update.

Which payment methods can I use?

This sample storefront does not process payments. Payment method and instructions—including any available card, PayPal, or Venmo options—will be confirmed securely with you when your order is arranged.

Can I order in another color or style?

The photos show the styles currently featured here. Contact us to ask about available colors, sizes, and current stock before placing an order.
